## Education

My education most notably includes an upper second-class honours in Computer Science from the University of Sussex. The degree provided knowledge of programming fundamentals, data structures, program analysis, operating systems, machine learning, databases and web computing among several others.

My dissertation involved evaluating techniques for caching and congestion control in Content Centric Networks (CCNs) to identify more efficient data flow between senders and receivers.

The main programming languages learnt during this time were Java and Python, using Git for version control.

## Employment

### Telefónica Tech UK (2018-2025)

#### Cloud and Infrastructure Engineer

After graduating from university I joined Telefónica Tech UK as a Cloud and Infrastructure Engineer. Within a public cloud, modern workplace and infrastructure delivery team, I supported senior consultants with client facing workshops, creating design documentation, writing infrastructure as code, deploying cloud infrastructure and training for users. Within a short time, the role involved independent workloads, working directly with clients and project managers.

Alongside client work, I provided internal development of IaC and DevOps processes which were used to standardise and significantly improve our delivery practices in public cloud.

In the final stage of this role, I transitioned to entirely internal development, creating API-backed, self-service Azure products for clients to consume via a web portal.

#### Cloud Automation Engineer

An internal opportunity allowed me to transition my role into development of automation used by both our managed service and delivery teams, with a company vision of creating a strong Cloud Centre of Excellence. The role involved running internal projects across multiple teams, writing IaC and automation (via pipelines/scripts) and educating teams on use of DevOps tools, agile processes and Azure configuration.
